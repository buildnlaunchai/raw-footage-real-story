# Bundled music beds — licensing

These three files are **procedurally synthesised**, not recordings. They were
generated with ffmpeg from oscillators and filtered noise (`sine`, `anoisesrc`,
`tremolo`, `lowpass`), using the exact commands recorded in
`src-tauri/resources/music/GENERATED.md`.

There is therefore no third-party recording, performance, composition or
sample involved, and no licence to honour. They are placed in the public
domain (CC0 1.0) as far as this project is concerned, and a distributor may
ship, modify or remove them freely.

## Why they are synthetic

Sourcing genuinely good royalty-free music requires downloading third-party
audio and verifying its licence, neither of which could be done responsibly
here. Rather than bundle files of uncertain provenance, these beds are simple
enough that their origin is not in question.

They are **ambient drones, not songs.** They do the job of sitting under a
voice without competing with it, and nothing more. If the product wants real
music, that is a sourcing task for a human — see the note in the project
report.

## If you replace them

Anything bundled in this directory must be CC0, public domain, or a licence
that explicitly permits redistribution inside a commercial product **and**
sublicensing to distributors. Creative Commons "BY" variants require
attribution that this app has nowhere sensible to display, and "NC" variants
are incompatible with the distribution model entirely.
